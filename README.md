**Last Updated: 29 October 2026**

## 1. Information We Handle

### a) Game Progress and Preferences

Crave Street: Food Empire may store certain information locally on your device to provide and maintain gameplay features, including:

- Food street and restaurant progress.
- Unlocked areas, food stations, counters, and machines.
- Worker progress and upgrades.
- In-game cash, gems, rewards, and other virtual currency.
- Offline earnings and reward availability.
- Gameplay progression and unlocked content.
- Sound, music, vibration, haptic, and other game preferences.

This information helps restore your game progress and settings between gameplay sessions.

The current version of the game does not require users to create an account or provide personal information such as a name, phone number, or email address to play.

We use **Google AdMob** to display advertisements within the game. Advertisements may include banner ads, interstitial ads, and optional rewarded advertisements.

Depending on your device settings, privacy choices, permissions, and applicable laws, Google and its advertising partners may collect or process information including:

- IP address.
- Approximate location derived from the IP address.
- Advertising identifiers.
- Device identifiers.
- Device and operating system information.
- App interactions.
- Advertisement views and interactions.
- Diagnostic information.
- Crash and performance information.

This information may be used to:

- Display advertisements.
- Measure advertising performance.

- Maintain and improve advertising services.

Google processes information according to its own privacy policies and terms.

### c) Support Communications

If you contact us by email or another available support method, we may receive:

- Your email address.
